About school

My first computer science course goes as far as my middle school (in french, "Collège") where I had Scratch and Robotic programming. I then went to high school where I pursued computer science (with Physics and Complementary mathematics) where I mostly developped in Python while seeing :

  • graph theory,
  • nodes and trees (binary search...)
  • linear data structures (lists, stacks, queues)
  • networking, sql basis, classes
  • complexity,
  • recursivity...

I then went completing the engineering bachelor "Developpement & Data" (3yrs, license equivalent) @ Efrei Panthéon Assas by obtaining the RNCP37873 certification (Cooloc).
